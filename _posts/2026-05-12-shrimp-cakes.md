# Ingredients

* 1 lb shrimp, shelled and deveined
* 1 cup panko bread crumbs
* 2 tbsp green onions, finely chopped
* 1/2 lemon, juiced and zested
* 2 tbsp mayonnaise
* 1/2 tsp:
    * paprika
    * onion powder
    * black pepper
* 1/4 cup olive oil (for cooking)
* broccoli (prepared as you like, for side)

# Instructions

1. Defrost and devein the shrimp if required.
2. Gently pulse the shrimp in a food processor until coarsely chopped, but not pureed.
3. In a large bowl, combine shrimp with bread crumbs, eggs, green onions, lemon juice / zest, mayonnaise, and spices.
4. Divide into 8 portions and form into patties.
5. Heat olive oil in a shallow pan over medium-high heat, and cook patties ~4 minutes a side, until cooked through. Cook in batches if required for smaller pans.
